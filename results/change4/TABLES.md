### Post-hoc: sign-preserving check of quoted numbers (added 20 Oct 2026; not pre-registered; no call)

The frozen quote check compares the quote with the source after removing punctuation, and then looks for the cited number in the quote, so a quote that changed 45 to -45 would pass. This re-check finds the source span the quote matched (punctuation kept) and requires the cited number, with its sign, in that span. It covers every text and question citation the frozen check accepted; a citation that fails it would have made its check unusable. CHANGELOG R53.
