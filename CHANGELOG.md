# Public research change history

This changelog summarizes the study’s implementation, experiments, amendments, and analyses. The complete registration and amendments are in `PREREGISTRATION.md`, historical snapshots and source-version metadata are in `provenance/`, and numerical results are in `results/`.

- R0–R19, 6–7 October 2026: study implementation and pilots; strict matching, number conventions, provider pins, reasoning requests, output-budget change to 8,192, repeated-clean variability reference, graph-blind comparator, signature rules and audit guide.
- R23–R26, 12 October: audit-label provenance corrected to GPT-drafted and author-reviewed for 47 conversations, with three author examples; registration frozen before main-run results.
- R27–R29, 12–13 October: host throttling; unusable program checks handled explicitly; retired Qwen3 8B replaced by 14B after other models' results were collected. The replacement is an amendment and narrows the model-size comparison.
- R30–R35, 14–17 October: post-hoc analyses; prospectively specified exploratory guarded prompt and second answerer; strict program rescoring; three verification outcomes; offline replacement; second annotator and sensitivity excluding author examples.
- R36–R41, 17–18 October: scoring audit and gold-informed format bound; revised-verifier protocol, sample and power check; three development versions, selected under the recorded stop rule.
- R42–R46, 18 October: final revised-verifier evaluation; body-429 retry correction; unsuccessful joint improvement outcome; post-hoc relaxed column-label scoring; result-level replies remain authoritative when identical requests received different outputs.
- R47–R49, 19–20 October: aggregate study account activity and BYOK cost check. Reported total US$31.40 includes pilots and duplicate calls and is not an incremental deployment cost.
- R50, 20 October: post-hoc own-history analysis, audited-graph sensitivity and counterfactual arithmetic-reuse signatures. These do not change confirmatory hypothesis calls.
- R53, 20 October: scientific-notation answer parser, substitution-site rule and citation-sign implementation corrections. Frozen rows remain intact; corrected-parser sensitivity is stored separately. No new model call.
- 22 October: research software, saved results, and reproducibility documentation packaged for release.
